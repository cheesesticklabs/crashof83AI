# AI lesson: classroom hand-in

Original lesson pages remain, followed by Your game & AI (page 10): six writing prompts about the creation process, Atari comparison, quality, consumer protection, spending, and predictions for next year and five years from now. English and Spanish are included.

Enter **ocarina** at the end of the modern examples (page 9) to open the reflection. Page 10 Continue opens the original exit ticket. Page 11 Continue opens download/review. Enter **freeplay** at the end of page 12 to open the arcade. Earlier passwords are unchanged. **atari5600** still unlocks every page.

Students enter name and period, download a PDF, then open the assignment in Google Classroom, choose Add or create → File, upload the PDF, and click Turn in. No Google Sheet save or receipt is needed. PDFs include all 19 questions, answers, identity, and page completion counts. Blank answers do not block downloading or navigation. This site does not mark the Classroom assignment as submitted.

The original Google Sheet scripts are retained as reference files; they are not used by the student workflow. The CS version is unchanged.
